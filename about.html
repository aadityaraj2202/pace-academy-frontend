OUR STORY

Building Careers. Shaping Futures.

Pace Academy, established in 2009 by Mr. Vivek Yadav, is a premier competitive examination coaching institute dedicated to providing quality education, expert guidance, and result-oriented preparation to aspirants across India.

Over the years, Pace Academy has built a strong reputation for thousands of successful selections in various competitive examinations. Our approach combines experienced faculty, structured study material, regular assessments, personalized guidance, and a focused learning environment to help students achieve their career goals.
